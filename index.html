CashPulse Rewards — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Version 2.5 · Google Play & GDPR Compliant

1. Information We Collect

CashPulse ("we", "our", or "the App") collects only the minimum data needed to verify reward legitimacy, prevent fraud, and deliver payouts:

  • Account & Wallet Identifiers: Email address (when registered or submitting a payout), Firebase Authentication UID, and anonymized player handle.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID), used for rewarded/interstitial ad delivery and partner offer attribution.
  • Playtime Usage Statistics (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Only when you explicitly grant Usage Access permission after our Prominent Disclosure, CashPulse and playtime mediation partners (such as adjoe GmbH) read foreground app package names and active gameplay duration of sponsored partner games installed through our Playtime/Game offers. We never read messages, screen content, or keystrokes.
  • Anti-Fraud Integrity Signals: Device hardware fingerprint and IP/network checks to block VPN, proxy, and emulator abuse.

2. Advertising & Offerwall Partners

CashPulse works with third-party advertising and offerwall partners including Google AdMob, Tapjoy, Inc., adjoe GmbH (Playtime), ayeT-Studios GmbH, and CPX Research. Each partner processes pseudonymous identifiers under its own privacy policy.

3. Age Requirement (18+ / COPPA)

CashPulse is intended solely for individuals aged 18 years or older (or the legal age of majority in their jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

4. Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account, wallet, receipts, and stored personal telemetry at any time inside the CashPulse app or by emailing cash.pulse.support@gmail.com.
